Campus life

Houses, clubs, sport, boarding and care.

The house system

On the first day of JSS1 each student joins one of four houses. Houses stay with students for six years and become a second family: older students mentor younger ones, house captains lead assemblies and every member contributes points for academic achievement, sports, music, drama, punctuality and tidiness.

At the end of each term the house with the most points is celebrated at assembly, and at the end of the year the winning house receives the Principal’s Shield. Friendly rivalry gives school life energy, and the house system makes sure every child, quiet or confident, has somewhere to belong.

Clubs and societies

Clubs meet on Wednesday afternoons, and students choose at least one each term.

  • Robotics and Coding
  • Debate and Public Speaking
  • Press and Writers
  • Junior Engineers
  • Young Entrepreneurs
  • Red Cross
  • Choir and Music
  • Drama
  • Art and Design
  • Chess
  • Environmental Club
  • Mathematics Circle

Each club has a staff patron and a student president. Clubs enter regional competitions, host talks, publish a termly magazine and organise service projects.

Sport and wellbeing

Physical education for all

Every student has timetabled PE each week and learns teamwork, discipline and healthy habits. Football, basketball, volleyball, athletics, table tennis and swimming days are all part of the programme.

Inter-house sports

Our annual sports festival fills the field with colour, with track and field events, relay races, tug of war and a march-past. Students of every ability take part.

Counselling and pastoral care

Form teachers know each student well, and our counsellor offers a safe, confidential space to talk about stress, friendships, family or future plans.

Health and nutrition

Our kitchen prepares balanced meals, with attention to allergies and dietary needs. The medical room is staffed throughout the school day and for boarders at night.

Boarding at Evason

Boarding students live in clean, well-ventilated houses under the care of resident house parents. A typical weekday begins with morning devotion and breakfast, followed by lessons, lunch, clubs or games, supervised evening prep, dinner and lights-out at a fixed time. Weekends include chapel or mosque visits, laundry, recreation, outings and phone time to speak with family.

Visiting days are published on the school calendar, and parents are welcome to contact house parents at any time with concerns or questions.

Safety and discipline

Our rules are few, clear and applied consistently. Students are expected to arrive on time, wear uniform properly, respect staff and each other, take care of property and be honest. Bullying, violence, drug use and cheating are not tolerated and are dealt with firmly, fairly and with parents informed.

Gates are controlled, visitors are signed in and every staff member undergoes background checks and child protection training. Students know exactly who to talk to if they feel unsafe, and every report is taken seriously.

Events through the year

Orientation week

New students tour the campus, meet their house and form teachers, and learn how to use the student portal.

Science and technology fair

Students present experiments, models and software projects to judges, parents and visitors.

Cultural day

A joyful celebration of Nigeria’s languages, music, dance, food and dress, planned and led by students.

Prize-giving and graduation

An evening to honour academic excellence, service and character, and to send SS3 graduates into the next chapter of their lives.
