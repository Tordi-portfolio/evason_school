A school where nobody is guessing how they are doing

Too many families wait until the end of the year to learn that a child has been struggling. We built Evason around the opposite idea: no surprises.

Each term your child sits two continuous assessment tests and one final examination in every subject. The two tests are marked out of 20, the examination out of 60, and together they make a total of 100. Teachers explain the marking scheme in the first week of term, so students know what is expected long before a paper is placed in front of them.

When marking is complete, our result officers enter the scores subject by subject against each student’s registration number. The system adds the marks, awards the grade, works out the average and the position in class, and then, only when the school chooses to publish, shows the result on the student’s own dashboard. Nobody else can open it.

Parents who prefer paper still receive a printed report. Those who prefer a screen can log in with their child and talk through every subject together.

What makes Evason different

Six commitments we make to every family who joins us.

Teaching that starts with understanding

Our teachers are trained to ask “why does this work?” before “what is the answer?”. Lessons are built around worked examples, short discussions and quick checks, so that a student who has not understood is noticed on the same day, not at the end of term.

Honest assessment, recorded properly

Scores are entered by named staff, stored securely and never overwritten without a trace. The grading scale is the same for every class and every subject, and it is printed in every student handbook.

A results portal families actually use

Every student has a personal account, created at registration. Results appear there as soon as they are published, with total, grade, remark, class average and position, ready to view on a phone or print for the file.

Technology as a working tool

From JSS1, students learn typing, spreadsheets, safe internet use and the basics of programming. In the senior classes they build real projects, from simple databases to small websites, using our computer laboratory and school-wide Wi-Fi.

Character as well as grades

Punctuality, respect, honesty and service are taught and rewarded. Prefects, house captains and club leaders are chosen by their peers and trained for the job, so leadership is practised and not just praised.

Safety you can see

Controlled gates, trained security staff, a resident nurse, supervised boarding houses and a clear child protection policy keep every student safe. Parents are told quickly and plainly whenever something needs their attention.

Two stages, one clear path

Junior Secondary, JSS1 to JSS3

Junior secondary is where good habits are set. Students study English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Social Studies, Civic Education, Computer Studies, Business Studies, Agricultural Science, Religious Studies, Cultural and Creative Arts, Physical and Health Education and a Nigerian language.

By JSS3 students sit the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with confidence, and meet our guidance counsellor to choose a senior school pathway that matches their strengths and ambitions.

See the junior curriculum

Senior Secondary, SS1 to SS3

In senior secondary students choose a pathway in Science, Arts or Commercial subjects, while continuing with English, Mathematics, Civic Education and Computer Studies. Classes are small, practicals are frequent and past-question practice begins early and continues steadily.

Our SS3 students sit the WAEC and NECO examinations and are prepared for JAMB UTME, post-UTME and scholarship tests through timetabled revision clinics and mock examinations.

Your child’s results, safe and private

The Evason student portal was designed with one rule at its heart: a student sees their own results and nobody else’s. Here is how it works in practice.

  1. Register and choose a classEach student registers online, picks their class from JSS1 to SS3 and receives a unique registration number.
  2. Teachers enter the scoresAuthorised staff select the student by registration number and enter the first test, second test and exam for each subject the class offers.
  3. The system does the arithmeticTotals, grades, remarks, averages and class positions are calculated automatically, with no copying errors.
  4. The school publishes, the student readsOnce results are published, the student logs in and finds them on a personal dashboard, ready to print.

Life beyond the timetable

Students learn best when they belong. At Evason every child is placed in one of four houses and competes for the year’s honours in academics, sports, drama, debate and cleanliness. Clubs meet every Wednesday afternoon, including Robotics, Press and Writers, Debate, Junior Engineers, Entrepreneurs, Red Cross, Music and Choir, and Art and Design.

Each term the school hosts an inter-house sports day, a cultural day and an open evening for parents. Every year students take part in community service, from tree planting to tutoring primary pupils in the neighbourhood.
